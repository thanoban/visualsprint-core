# Competitor evidence

Research date: 2026-10-11. Capabilities below are public claims/documentation, not independent
performance certification. Internal vendor databases and private deployment topology are unknown.

| Product | Public evidence | Design implication |
|---|---|---|
| Fathom | [Teams bot restrictions](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/13114369); [API/webhooks](https://developers.fathom.ai/index) | Admission and capture need distinct states; public integration contracts are baseline |
| Fireflies | [GraphQL, webhooks, realtime](https://docs.fireflies.ai/getting-started/introduction) | Event-driven integrations and accessible meeting data |
| Granola | [Device capture](https://www.granola.ai/blog/how-to-use-granola-with-zoom); [scoped meeting chat](https://docs.granola.ai/help-center/getting-more-from-your-notes/chatting-with-your-meetings) | Cross-meeting/customer context already exists; device coverage is a separate form factor |
| Otter | [Calendar and ad-hoc capture](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/13674910923671-Automatically-add-Otter-Notetaker-to-your-meetings) | One durable pipeline for scheduled and pasted links |
| tl;dv | [MCP and cross-meeting workflows](https://intercom.help/tldv/en/articles/15322415-mcp) | Permission-aware external retrieval interface |
| Avoma | [Meeting data API](https://help.avoma.com/api-documentation) | Customer intelligence and connector extensibility |
| Read AI | [Connected search](https://support.read.ai/hc/en-us/articles/39009378777875-Using-Ask-Read-to-search-your-meetings-and-connected-apps); [actions](https://support.read.ai/hc/en-us/articles/48863788829971-Using-Ask-Read-actions) | Follow-through is competitive territory; measure our quality |
| Gong | [Conversation intelligence](https://www.gong.io/conversation-intelligence) | Customer continuity, with broader internal-team support in our product |
| Krisp | [Bot-free transcription](https://krisp.ai/meeting-transcription/zoom/) | Device audio is outside our launch form factor |
| Tactiq | [Browser transcription](https://tactiq.io/speech-to-text) | Attended extension capture is different from unattended bots |
| Google Meet | [Artifact API](https://developers.google.com/workspace/meet/api/guides/artifacts) | Imports depend on generated/authorized transcripts |
| Teams | [Transcript API](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/platform/graph-api/meeting-transcripts/overview-transcripts) | Permissions and transcript availability are separate from calendar connection |
| Zoom | [RTMS quickstart](https://godevelopers.zoom.us/docs/rtms/meetings/quickstart-rest-api/) | Native realtime integrations are future qualified adapters |

Do not infer that competitors lack project chat, agendas, commitments or integrations merely
because we have not tested them. Maintain verified / vendor-documented / inferred / unknown labels.
Claims about reliability, setup time and quality require comparative tests with the same scenarios.

Our hypothesis: source-linked reviewed commitments, exact-payload actions, verified status and
next-meeting preparation can reduce founder context loss. Pilot metrics: missed commitments,
time to prepare, correction rate, task duplication, unauthorized disclosures, capture gaps and
cost per meeting hour. Architecture alone does not prove a competitive advantage.
